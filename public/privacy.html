Word Climb Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Word Climb does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information, from children or anyone else.

  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services.
  • The app makes no network requests while you play.
  • Reading progress (which cards have been read and how each read went) is saved only on the device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • On Android, the operating system's own device backup may include this local data if backup is turned on in the device settings. That backup is controlled by the device owner and Google, not by Word Climb.

Word Climb is open source, so you can check this yourself: github.com/iCodeForBananas/word-climb. Questions can be raised as an issue there.
